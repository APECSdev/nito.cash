The Story of Nito Cash

The 2026 upgrade let a transaction
verify a zk-proof by itself…

…so we asked: what if a stablecoin payment could look like nothing at all?
The pool is an open community resource: a covenant that holds the tokens, a Groth16 circuit that hides the payers, and wallets that prove everything in the browser. No backend. No custodian. Any wallet can integrate.

Benefits of Nito Cash

Why shield your PUSD?

  • Payments that look like nothing — sender, receiver & amount masked
  • Variable amounts — no fixed denominations, no round-number tells
  • Keys never leave your device — BIP39/BIP32 + Schnorr, all local
  • Notes sealed with AES-256-GCM; only ciphertext ever persists
  • 10x less chain-snooping!
