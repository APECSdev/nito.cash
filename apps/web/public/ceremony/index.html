Nito.cash — Trusted Setup Ceremony

Private Payments (PUSD shielded pool on Bitcoin Cash Chipnet)

The Nito.cash shielded pool uses Groth16 (over the BN254 curve), which requires a trusted setup. The setup has two phases. This page records exactly what was run, what is reused, and what is still to come. It is plain, wallet-free text on purpose: the ceremony record must be readable without opening the wallet.

Phase 1 — Powers of Tau (reused, public)

The Powers of Tau ceremony is circuit-agnostic (it depends only on the curve and the maximum circuit size), so we reuse an existing, publicly attested ceremony: the Perpetual Powers of Tau (Privacy & Scaling Explorations, ongoing since 2019, 86 participants, security rooted in the Zcash Powers of Tau).

Concretely we use the file ppot_0080_18.ptau (2^18 = 262,144 points, covering our 183,469 constraints; 302,083,218 bytes), prepared from contribution #0080 plus a public Ethereum-beacon randomness beacon (slot 7,325,000, 31 iterations). Because contributions are sequential, it transitively includes every earlier contribution — among them #0023, the nito.cash operator's own (attested March 6–8, 2020; see 0023_shomari_response in the ceremony repo). The file's structure was verified byte-by-byte (magic, BN254 prime, power 18, ceremony power 28) and it is consumed correctly by snarkjs.

Phase 2 — circuit-specific (our own)

The Phase 2 ceremony is specific to our circuit and must be our own. This is where the toxic-waste assumption lives: as long as at least one contributor is honest and destroys their randomness, the setup is secure.

Chipnet PoC — disclosed 1-of-1. For the Chipnet proof-of-concept we ran a single local 1-of-1 contribution (one contributor, on the build VPS). This is acceptable for a valueless chipnet demo and is fully disclosed — it is not a decentralized ceremony and must not be treated as one.

Mainnet — full MPC mandatory. Before any mainnet deployment holding real value, a multi-party computation (MPC) ceremony with multiple independent, publicly-attesting contributors is mandatory. A 1-of-1 setup is never acceptable for real funds, because a single compromised contributor could forge proofs and drain the pool. The community ceremony will be announced here.
